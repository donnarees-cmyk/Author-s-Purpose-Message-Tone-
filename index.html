📚 1 · Learn It

Why do authors write? There are five big purposes for writing:

🖼️ Describe
Paint a picture with words.
📚 Inform
Teach facts and explain.
📖 Narrate
Tell a story.
📣 Persuade
Convince the reader.
🎭 Entertain
Amuse or delight the reader.
Autobiography = the story of the writer's own life. Its main purpose is to narrate. But by telling their life story, authors also share a central idea (message), an insight about the events. Word choice also shows the author's tone: their attitude toward the subject.

Look for clues about purpose, message, and tone:

1. Title
What idea does it stress?
2. Beginning and end
Does the author state an idea outright?
3. Word choice
Is the language figurative, funny, dramatic, formal, serious, or direct?
💡 Tip: Sometimes the writer simply states the purpose and message. More often, you notice details, think about them, and draw your own conclusions.
